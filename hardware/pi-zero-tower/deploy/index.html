Thanks for printing this! It's 5 parts in black & white PLA: 4 identical trays and 1 lid. Everything you need is on this page.

1 · Download

Each part is a white file plus a black file.

tray_white.stl tray_black.stl lid_white.stl lid_black.stl

2 · Load into the slicer

  • Import tray_white.stl and tray_black.stl together and answer Yes to “load as a single object with multiple parts”.
  • Do the same with lid_white.stl + lid_black.stl.
  • Assign white filament to the _white parts and black to the _black parts.
  • Make 4 copies of the tray and keep 1 lid. All 5 fit on one plate.

3 · Settings

  • PLA, white + black
  • 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20–25% infill
  • No supports. The openings are short bridges.
  • Don't scale or rotate. The holes are sized for specific screws, and orientation is already set: trays print floor-down, and the lid prints top-down so the black text is the first layers.
5parts (4 trays + lid)
